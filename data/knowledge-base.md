## SECTION 1: WHO WE ARE

Mortar Metrics is a legal marketing agency. We help law firms get more signed cases through digital marketing. Not leads — signed, retained cases. Every lawyer has been burned by agencies that deliver "leads" that never convert. We solve that.

Founded by Fardeen. Based in Toronto. Serve law firms across the US, Canada, and UK. Practice area agnostic — we work with any law firm: personal injury, family law, criminal defense, immigration, estate planning, employment, bankruptcy, anything.

## SECTION 2: HOW WE OPERATE (the real picture — gaps and all)

### Current Numbers (February 2026)

- **MRR:** ~$20K (with pending deals)
- **Paying clients:** 4-7
- **Close rate:** ~10% or less (this needs to improve dramatically)
- **Report-to-call conversion:** ~50%
- **Cold email volume:** ~15K/day via Instantly
- **Discovery calls/week:** Inconsistent (some weeks good, some dry)
- **Proposal turnaround:** Too slow (should be same day, often isn't)
- **Average time to close:** Up to a month (goal: compress to 1 week)
- **Goal:** $100K MRR within 6 months

### The Math: $20K → $100K MRR
- Target: ~20-25 clients at $4-5K average
- Need: ~15-18 new clients in 6 months = ~3 new clients/month
- At 10% close rate: need 30 discovery calls/month = ~7-8/week
- At 20% close rate (with better process): need 15 calls/month = ~4/week
- **The four levers:** Volume (more calls), Conversion (better close rate), Speed (compress cycle), Retention (stop churn)
- Every feature, brief, and recommendation connects to one of these four levers

### The #1 Bottleneck
More meetings booked. That's it. The whole funnel leaks — not enough calls, close rate is low (~10%), deals ghost after proposals — but the root problem is volume at the top.

### The Sales Funnel (how it actually works today)

Cold Email (Instantly, ~15K/day) + LinkedIn DMs (Prosp)
→ Lead replies interested
→ Revenue Rescue Report auto-generates (custom marketing audit)
→ Yaseer calls within 5 minutes (IN THEORY — sometimes slower)
→ If no pickup: tries again, but NO systematic retry schedule
→ 7-email nurture sequence follows the report
→ Discovery call (Yaseer wings it — no script, no deck, no standard questions)
→ After call: Yaseer adds them on WhatsApp/SMS
→ Sends proposal via email AND WhatsApp
→ WhatsApp nurture: sends value, gets them excited
→ Follow-up until close or ghost (NO systematic pestering schedule)
→ Client signs → fulfillment team onboards (should be 1 week, sometimes longer)

### What's MISSING from the funnel

1. No speed-to-lead tracking. We say "5 minutes" but don't measure it.
2. No retry schedule when leads don't pick up.
3. No discovery call script or framework. Yaseer wings it every time.
4. No sales deck or visual presentation with ROI calculation.
5. No ROI calculator for proposals.
6. No pestering system between stages. Every stage should have 15 touches.
7. No objection playbook. Handling is improvised. Real objections live in Fireflies.
8. No WhatsApp tracking. WhatsApp is core but invisible to the system.

### Why We Lose Deals
- NOT to other agencies (rarely)
- NOT to in-house hires
- People like the product but don't buy because:
  - Didn't sell hard enough / didn't prove value at the price point
  - Pricing — they don't see the value for the price
  - Offer and guarantee weren't strong enough (improving — risk reversal is new)
  - No systematic objection handling
  - No systematic follow-up after proposals

### Why Clients Churn
- Bad communication on our end
- Results not great enough
- This is why $10K/month matters: better price → better fulfillment → better results → less churn

### The Offer (constantly being tested)
Current: "We'll guarantee you $30K in 30 days or we work for free" (or similar).
Testing different versions based on what gets cold email replies.
The offer and guarantee are THE differentiator.

### The Guarantee Structure
- Full price = full guarantee (specific results, specific cases, specific timeframe)
- Discounted price = no guarantee
- Currently loose — Yaseer sometimes makes up numbers in the meeting
- Want: ROI calculator that builds the guarantee naturally during the presentation

### WhatsApp as a Sales Channel
After every discovery call, Yaseer asks for WhatsApp or SMS. Adds them to group chat or direct message. Sends:
- Proposal (email AND WhatsApp)
- Value messages every few days ("We looked at firms in your area, you're so close to winning")
- Nurturing to keep them excited between calls
Goal: they never forget us. Every touchpoint from A to Z.

### The Whiteboard Session (what Command Centre replaces)
Every morning (when it happens — inconsistent), Fardeen:
1. Looks at everything going on
2. Identifies the biggest problem / bottleneck
3. Tells team: "This is what's broken. This is what needs to be done today."
4. Assigns specific tasks
5. Next day: check in on what got done
Problem: inconsistent. People go on autopilot doing busywork. Command Centre must do this every day, automatically, with real data.

### Pestering Vision (5 stages)
Stage 1 (interested → report_sent): 7-email nurture (EXISTS), Yaseer calls 5 min
Stage 2 (report_sent → call_booked): NEEDS BUILDING. Retry at day 2/4/6 across phone/WhatsApp/LinkedIn
Stage 3 (call_booked → discovery): NEEDS BUILDING. Pre-call excitement messages
Stage 4 (discovery → proposal): CRITICAL. Proposal SAME DAY. Alert at 4h, escalate at 24h
Stage 5 (proposal → closed/lost): WHERE DEALS DIE. Day 1-10 sequence across WhatsApp/email/phone
Goal: compress entire cycle from 1 month to 1 week.

### Wave Fee Pricing (from Hormozi Money Models)
- **Option A:** $20,000 setup + $7,500/month (12-month commitment)
- **Option B (recommended):** $0 setup + $10,000/month (12-month commitment, 90-day exit clause)
- Nobody picks A. B feels like $20K free. 90-day exit = psychological 3-month commit.
- Ad spend NOT included — $3,000-$5,000/month on their own account (separate)

### The Guarantee
"Minimum [X] booked, qualified consultations on your calendar within 90 days. If we don't hit it — and you completed onboarding within 7 days and maintained agreed ad budget — exit with no wave fee, last month refunded."
- Guarantee CONSULTATIONS not clients (we control intake, not their closing)
- "Qualified" defined in contract (expressed intent, within practice area)
- Conditional: they must hold up their end (ad account access, 48h response to setup requests)
- This is the guarantee that doesn't kill us: we control what we guarantee

### The Sales Presentation Sequence (Hormozi Close)
1. Discovery — understand pain, get their numbers (5 min)
2. Revenue Rescue — show money they're losing, dollar figure (5 min)
3. Live Demo — call the AI agent, let them hear it (3 min)
4. Value Stack — walk through everything, show $21,500 total (5 min)
5. Bonuses — stack on top, $9,500 additional value (2 min)
6. ROI Math — plug their numbers in, show buying money at discount (2 min)
7. Cost Comparison — show in-house cost, make $10K feel cheap (1 min)
8. Price Reveal — present both options, let them choose (2 min)
9. Guarantee — remove last objection (1 min)
10. Close — "Which option makes more sense for your firm?" Then silence. (1 min)

**NEVER present price before value. NEVER discount — add bonuses instead.**

## SECTION 4: COLD EMAIL FRAMEWORK

### Ben's 5-Step Sequence
1. Frontend offer (report push or meeting push)
2. Insights/ideas
3. Case study/social proof (Mandall Law)
4. Objection handling
5. Final value drop

### Rules
- Under 50 words ALWAYS
- No em dashes ever
- Space between every line
- Light spintax — spin full phrases not individual words
- Every email: dollar amount + Mandall Law proof
- Lead with THEIR problem not YOUR solution
- "every 30 days" not "month"
- "clients"/"cases" for results, not "leads"
- Mandall Law proof in P.S.
- "Say the word"/"Let me know" CTAs
- No process talk (don't explain how we do things)
- First name only personalization
- "we" not "I" for brand consistency

### Spam Words to AVOID
Group 1: generate, additional, month, reply, new, bring in, extra, add, obtain, secure, respond, free, report, guaranteed, growth, land, ad spend
Group 2: follow up with, how to fix it, get back to, what to do, contact, call back, how much, handle it, signed cases, solve it, just say sure, turn it around, at no cost, deliver, cases, pay, nothing, call

### What's Working
- Competitor gap angle + freebie reports
- Leading with Mandall Law proof as opener PASSES spam checkers better than generic dollar claims
- Dollar claims attached to a real firm read as FACT not pitch
- Selling the call as a valuable deliverable ("I'll walk you through exactly what we found") = 3x more bookings than generic CTAs

### Email Checklist (every email must pass)
1. Spintax applied
2. Spam word list checked
3. All permutations read naturally
4. Conversion checked per Ben's framework
5. Has dollar amount + Mandall Law proof

## SECTION 5: REPORT DESIGN PRINCIPLES (Revenue Rescue Report)

- Lead with THEIR problem (verifiable), not YOUR solution (unbelievable)
- Cases first, dollars secondary ("30 signed cases" hits harder than "$92K")
- Conservative ranges — "believable ambition" not fantasy numbers
- Guarantee visually dominant
- Kill AI-speak
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Plain English — passes the "Frank test" (would a 60-year-old lawyer understand and book?)
- Numbers scale by market (Lincoln NE ≠ Orange County CA)
- Each practice area has different economics
- Cost-of-inaction section (show what doing nothing costs)
- Investment/ROI info upfront to reduce sticker shock before sales call

## SECTION 7: WHAT THE COMMAND CENTRE MUST BE

### It's not a dashboard. It's the operating brain.

1. REPLACE the whiteboard session. Every morning: bottleneck, tasks per person, numbers.
2. HANDHOLD Yaseer. "Call Kyle Kinsey NOW — here's his number, what he said, the objection he'll raise, how to handle it, the proposal link."
3. PESTER the team. 15 messages per stage. Nag via Telegram until done.
4. TRACK everything invisible. Speed to lead. Days since contact. Deal stages.
5. THINK and ADVISE. "Close rate dropped to 8%. Deals with same-day proposals close at 18% vs 4%. Fix proposal speed."
6. BUILD things. Identify gaps, build fixes or tell Fardeen exactly what to build.
7. LEARN continuously. Every call, deal, approval, rejection, correction = smarter.
8. ASK QUESTIONS. "How did Kyle Kinsey go?" Every answer makes it smarter.

### How it talks
- Names names. "Call Kyle Kinsey" not "follow up with prospects"
- Gives deadlines. "TODAY before 5 PM" not "soon"
- References real data. "Kyle said he's spending $8K on ads with no tracking"
- Thinks in revenue. "$4K/month deal dies Friday if nobody calls"
- Assigns to right person. Closing → Yaseer. Content → Monty/Juhi. Systems → Fardeen.
- Flags the bottleneck. "Top of funnel is down. Email reply rate dropped to 0.8%."
- Pesters. Again. And again. Until it's done.
- Advises. "You're undercharging again. This deal should be $5K not $3K."
- Asks questions to learn.

### Tone
Senior agency consultant who's seen everything. Direct. Numbers. Specifics. No fluff. No "I hope this helps." Treats Fardeen's time as the most valuable resource. Speed = money.
